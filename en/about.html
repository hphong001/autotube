01A message from our founder

Over 20 years as an engineer and as a manufacturing business owner, the scene I saw most often was “good equipment and good software, with people still filling the gaps between them.”

Copying measurements onto paper, switching between three programs, watching monitors through the night.

It was the same when producing Korean customers' products in Chinese factories and handling quality claims. So I started building the tools myself — and those tools became systems used on production lines and in offices at many clients.

At AI BASE LAB, the same engineer is responsible from the first conversation through design, development, on-site installation and maintenance. Requirements don't get lost along the way, and problems get solved quickly.

For overseas companies, dealing with Korean factory environments, strict MES requirements, and language barriers can be painful. We are not just interpreters. We are true engineers who understand both your product's technical depth and your Korean customer's site. We bridge that gap seamlessly.

Founder & Lead Engineer, AI BASE LAB
