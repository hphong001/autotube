Privacy Policy

AI BASE LAB (“we”) protects the personal information of people who contact us, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. The Korean version of this policy prevails in case of any difference.

1. Information we collect

  • Required: company name, your name, email address, country, requested services, product or system
  • Optional: company website, job title, phone number, city, messenger ID, project details, how you found us
  • Collected automatically: date and time of the inquiry, the page it was sent from

2. How we use it

Only to answer your inquiry, arrange meetings, and send quotations or proposals. We do not use it for advertising.

3. How long we keep it

For 3 years from the date of the inquiry, then deleted without delay. We delete it immediately if you ask us to. If a contract is signed, records are kept for the period required by law.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your information to third parties unless you consent or the law requires it.

5. Processors and international transfer

Inquiries are stored in Google Sheets and notification emails are sent via Gmail (Google LLC). Your information may therefore be stored in countries where Google operates data centers, including the United States. If you do not agree to this, please email us directly at contact@aibaselab.com instead of using the form.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information at any time by emailing contact@aibaselab.com.

7. Security

  • Access to inquiry data limited to the person responsible for privacy
  • Accounts protected with two-step verification
  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS)

8. Cookies and analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how the website is used. It collects non-identifying visit information through cookies. You can refuse cookies in your browser settings.

9. Contact

Person responsible for privacy: Representative · contact@aibaselab.com

10. Effective date

This policy applies from October 4, 2026. Korean version
