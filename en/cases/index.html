CASE 01 · EQUIPMENT INTEGRATION · MONITORING

Three measuring instruments, one inspection program

Audio equipment manufacturer · outgoing inspection station · audio analyzer · DC power supply · barcode scanner · LTE / RS-232 / USB / LAN

Challenge

  • For every unit tested, operators switched between a separate program for each instrument.
  • Measurements were written on paper and re-typed into Excel after each shift.
  • Someone had to walk out to the line to see how testing was going.

What we built

  • One program controlling all instruments over LTE, RS-232, USB and LAN (SCPI) — scan, measure, judge and record in a single flow.
  • Serial number, measurements and pass/fail saved to Excel automatically for every unit.
  • Live charts with recording, data recall for trend and defect analysis.
  • Telegram alerts for pass rate, defects and equipment errors, with remote commands limited to approved accounts.

Results

  • Handwritten records and re-typing into Excel were no longer needed.
  • Test history can be found by serial number to answer customer questions.
  • Testing can be followed from the office, or from anywhere outside the plant.
  • Accumulated data is used to analyse defect trends and improve the process.
CASE 02 · QA INSPECTION · VISION · MES

Outgoing inspection: barcode, weight and camera checks before release

Manufacturer's shipping line · inspection station · barcode scanner · industrial scale · industrial camera · signal tower · MES server (REST API · DB)

Challenge

  • Wrong products or quantities were sometimes packed and shipped.
  • Boxes occasionally left the line with missing components or labels.
  • No record of product condition at shipment, and production counts were entered into the MES by hand, often late.

What we built

  • Scanned codes compared with the order, with sound and screen alerts on a mismatch.
  • Industrial scale integration checks weight against tolerance to catch missing parts.
  • Rule-based camera checks for labels and parts — no AI training data needed.
  • Photos stored per order and serial; counts and progress sent to the MES in real time.

Results

  • Wrong items and missing parts are caught before they are shipped.
  • Photos from shipment are available as evidence for customer claims.
  • Production counts reach the MES automatically.
  • Managers follow line progress from the office.
