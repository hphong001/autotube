FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you support our customers on-site in Korea?

Yes. We visit your customer's site anywhere in Korea — the Seoul area and nationwide — on a schedule agreed with you, and send a report after each visit in English or the format you prefer.

Can you work under our company name?

Yes, if you prefer. We can represent your company to your Korean customer as your local engineer, so your brand stays in front and the customer relationship stays with you.

Do you offer emergency response?

Emergency response is available to monthly support clients. Because we already know your product and the customer's site, we can act quickly when something goes wrong. For one-off requests, we schedule a visit as soon as we can.

What systems can you integrate with?

Measuring instruments, PLCs, barcode scanners, industrial scales and cameras over RS-232/485, USB and LAN (SCPI, Modbus, TCP), as well as MES, databases and REST APIs used by Korean manufacturers.

Which languages do you work in?

Technical meetings are held in English, and Korean is our native language. Professional interpreters, including Chinese, are available on request for important meetings.

How are fees set?

We review your requirements, agree on the engagement model — per project, monthly support or daily rate — and send a quotation. Travel and accommodation are charged at cost.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We can sign an NDA before you share any technical details.
