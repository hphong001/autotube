FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you support our customers on-site in Korea?

Yes. We visit your customer's site in Korea for installation, commissioning, troubleshooting and regular checks, and send you a report in English after each visit.

Can you work under our company name?

Yes, if you prefer. We can represent your company to your Korean customer as your local engineer, so your brand stays in front and the customer relationship stays with you.

What kinds of systems can you integrate?

Measuring instruments, PLCs, barcode scanners, industrial scales and cameras over RS-232/485, USB, LAN (SCPI, Modbus, TCP), as well as MES, databases and REST APIs used by Korean manufacturers.

How do we start working together?

Send us a short description of your product, your customer and what you need. We review it and reply by email, then agree on scope and a support model that fits you — per project, ongoing, or on-call.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We can sign an NDA before you share any technical details, and source code can be delivered according to the contract.
