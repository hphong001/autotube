ABOUT US

An engineer who knows the factory floor,
working for you in Korea

AI BASE LAB is a Korean engineering company that supports overseas companies with their customers in Korea — on site, in meetings and in software. Our work is built on 20+ years of product development, production and software engineering.

01A message from our lead engineer

I worked as an engineer at a major Korean electronics manufacturer, on IT products from planning and development through to mass production. I also have more than ten years of experience leading electronics sourcing and production in China.

On both sides, I saw the same thing again and again: good products held back by small gaps — a missing local engineer, a requirement lost in translation, a site visit that took a week to arrange.

Today we build automation software for Korean manufacturers, and we use that same experience to support overseas companies in Korea. We go to your customer's site, sit in their meetings, understand what they need and, when necessary, build it ourselves.

We would rather start small and earn your trust than promise everything at once.

Lead Engineer, AI BASE LAB

02Background
